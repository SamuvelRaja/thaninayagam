## Typography

Self-hosted Google Fonts: Kavivanar for Tamil calligraphic headings (SIL Open Font License) and Permanent Marker for English brush headings (Apache 2.0). English headings use the font’s own painted letterforms without an added displacement or dilation filter. Body copy keeps the existing reading fonts. `ink-stroke.svg` is a native decorative vector.

Permanent Marker is served locally from `public/assets/fonts/brush-display-3.woff2`; its license is `public/assets/fonts/permanent-marker-LICENSE.txt`. It is preloaded and is the default English display font, including English document titles on Tamil routes.

### Final design settings

English display headings use Permanent Marker and the primary colour is bright blue (#1558e8). The font and colour preview widgets and their stored-preference scripts have been removed. The blue paint filter remains available independently of those controls.
