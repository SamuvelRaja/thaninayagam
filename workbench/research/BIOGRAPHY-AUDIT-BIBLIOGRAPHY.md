# Biography audit: bibliography metadata and supporters biography

Audit date: 8 October 2026. Website files were read only. This report is the only file created by this audit.

The sole historical source is `public/books/TVA_BOK_0009080_தனிநாயகம்_அடிகளார்.md`, abbreviated **B** below. B identifies the book as *தனிநாயகம் அடிகளார்*, by Fr. Dr. Amudhan Adigal, International Institute of Tamil Studies, first edition July 1993 (B, unnumbered title/bibliographical material, lines 1–25). Every source reference below gives the printed-page marker in that Markdown and its line number. Front-matter Arabic markers 30/31 are the converter's rendering of Roman-numbered preliminary pages; the author's introduction explicitly retains xxxiii/xxxiv.

Scope: all 25 `WORKS` entries in `lib/data.ts`; title, date, authorship/editorial attribution, page counts and historical descriptions in all 50 English/Tamil document front matters; `components/ui/TributeHero.tsx`; the historical tribute wording in `app/sponsors/page.tsx` and `app/en/sponsors/page.tsx`. Digitization/access status, OCR quality, present-day payment details and institutional promises are outside this historical-source audit. Front-matter summaries describing substitute/related reading material are considered only for their historical identity, not whether the reading copy exists.

Verdicts are field-specific. **Supported** means B supplies the claim. **Contradicted** means B directly assigns a different fact to the same item/event. **Not located** means no evidence for that claim was found in B; it does not establish that the claim is false. **Ambiguous** means edition, pagination, work identity or an inconsistency within B prevents a firm conclusion. A later edition date is not automatically a contradiction of an earlier publication date. Scan leaves are not interchangeable with numbered text pages.

## Main findings

- The bibliography merges work dates, lecture dates, edition dates and journal-article dates. The most consequential cases are *Aspects of Tamil Humanism* (1961 Sornammal lectures versus the site's 1972 Bunker description), *Indian Thought and Roman Stoicism* (1962 delivery versus 1963 publication), and Proença's dictionary (1964 introductory articles versus 1966 dictionary edition).
- B dates *Landscape and Poetry* to 1966. The site consistently labels it 1967 without identifying a later edition. B explicitly connects the 1952/1953 *Nature in Ancient Tamil Poetry*, the 1963 *Nature Poetry in Tamil*, and the 1966 *Landscape and Poetry* as editions in the same research lineage. They should not be described as three unrelated studies.
- B supports *Tiruvalluvar* as three February 1961 lectures published in 1967 and describes a 60-page book. The site's 64 pages need an edition/pagination explanation; the English summary makes 1967 sound like the delivery date.
- B supports the 1966 *Reference Guide*, University of Malaya Press, 122 numbered pages, and its bibliography function. It supplies fuller metadata: subtitle *Books*, six preliminary pages, 1,355 book records, compilation begun 1961/completed 1963 and publication in 1966.
- The claim that Amudhan spent 30 years/over three decades collecting papers to produce the 1993 biography is **not located**. His own introduction says he met Thani Nayagam in 1965, thanks providers of books/articles and interviewees, and describes the memorial/endowment work. It does not specify a 30-year research period. Birthplace and 1943 birth year are also not verified by B.
- B was published in 1993. It cannot establish the 1995, 1999 or 2013 compilation metadata. Those entries need separate sources rather than a false/contradicted label.

> Rev. Dr. Amudhan Adigal wrote *தனிநாயகம் அடிகளார்*, published by the International Institute of Tamil Studies, Chennai, in July 1993. ## Source limitations to preserve

1. **Nature study date:** B itself gives 1952 in the narrative and bibliography, but 1953 in the chronological writings appendix. This is an internal source conflict, not proof the website's 1953 record is wrong.
2. **Tamil Studies Abroad pages:** B gives 268 in the narrative and vii+269 in the bibliography. Its evidence does not fix an exact total including preliminaries. The website's 288 needs independent edition/pagination evidence.
3. **Landscape pagination:** `vii, ISL p.` is visibly corrupted OCR. Do not turn it into a confident 151/157-page correction from this Markdown alone.
4. **Printed pages versus scans:** Website counts such as 157 leaves and 114/64 pages may reflect different conventions or editions. The audit reports B's conventions and flags the missing explanation rather than assuming they are false.
5. **Later editions/collections:** An earlier date in B does not exclude a later edition. Entries after July 1993 cannot be assessed historically by this source.
6. **Derivative reading pages:** A related-essay dossier can be useful, but its title/kind/year must not silently identify it as the full monograph, unpublished thesis or delivered lecture. This audit did not inspect reader completeness or digitization status.
