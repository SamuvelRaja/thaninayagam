## A. IATR — 1968 proceedings

**To:** president.iatrofficial@gmail.com  
**Cc:** (optional) General Secretary contact via https://iatrofficial.org/contact/  
**Subject:** Permission request — Xavier S. Thani Nayagam / 1966 Conference proceedings on a public research archive

Dear President Arullallar and colleagues,

I am preparing a bilingual (Tamil/English) public research archive devoted to Rev. Dr. Xavier S. Thani Nayagam Adigal’s scholarly work for Tamil. The site already links to the Tamil Digital Library and Internet Archive records for *Proceedings of the First International Conference-Seminar of Tamil Studies* (Kuala Lumpur, April 1966; published 1968) and hosts only a previously limited title-page illustration for identification.

The 1968 volume states that copyright is reserved to I.A.T.R. We therefore seek written permission for one or more of the following, in ascending scope:

1. Continued hosting of the title-page image with credit and outbound link;
2. Publication of short, attributed, human-reviewed excerpts from his own contributions — “Chairman's Introductory Remarks” (printed pp. 3–5) and “The Identification of Kataaram” (printed pp. 67–71);
3. Broader reuse of reviewed text or additional page images from the volume for non-commercial educational display.

We will not present unreviewed OCR as reliable text. If you prefer a different rights-holder contact, please redirect us.
