Doctor R. P. Sethupillai as a scholar, critic, and
writer wielded a great influence in the Tamil country.
during the years he was associated with the Universi-
ties of Tamil Nad. He was not an original thinker and
Philosopher like Thiru Vi.Ka. He was not a controver-
sialist and commentator and pioneer of Tamil prose like
Maraimalai Adigal, nor a research scholar like T. P.
Meenatchisundaram. Nor did he possess the creative
versatility of his successor to the Chair of Tamil, Dr. M.
Varadarajan. Above all R.P.S. was a fountain of Tamil
learning. He was a brilliant conversationalist with an
inexhaustible fund of narrative and episodes. He was
the University Professor who chose to take his learning
to the market place, and to imbue the Tamil country
with a love for the land and for its literature. His
memory will be long cherished by those who have
known and loved him, by those who have heard his
inimitable Tamil eloquence, and by all those who read
his mellifluous prose. |
