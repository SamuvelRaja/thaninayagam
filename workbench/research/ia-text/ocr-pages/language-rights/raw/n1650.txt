_ A full, integrated and equal partnership and citizen-
ship and intelligent Tamil participation in Government
demands a recognition of Tamil language rights. No State
has ever found it of advantage to have a permanently dis-
contented minority within the body politic. The reason-
able use of Tamil demands that Tamil also be made a
national and official language, and if justice demands that
a concession be made to the sentiments of the majority,
let Sinhalese be made the first language of the State and
Tamil-the second language of the State. But let both lan-
guages have a national and official status though in func-
tion the official use ‘of each language may be restricted to
