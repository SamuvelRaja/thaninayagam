The next book would be THE TEN STEPS,—a manual
of instructions for the conquest of the self and the appre-
hension of the one Reality. The ten steps are:—
1. Discrimination of the Real, 2. Initiation in the Divine
Ground, 3. Dying to sense and self, 4. The soul’s purifica-
tion, 5. Rendering a fit return, 6. Cleansing the heart for
receiving the Spirit, 7. Overwhelming sense of the Divine
Compassion, 8. Mystic Union, 9. Eestatic Bliss, 10. Over-
flow of Bliss. These are the ten steps of the Gnana-marga
shown in the Tirucchadagam. They lead to a great victory,
but, like all victories, it leaves a gnawing dread of loss in
the heart of the victor. An overwhelming sense of
unworthiness and a sinking fear of being forsaken are
intermingled with the overflow of bliss and evoke a heart-
rending entreaty whose burden is ‘Forsake-Me-Not’.
