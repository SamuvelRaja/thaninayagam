this question, which I confess made me waver many times, I reply that in
the other alphabets there are not the perplexity and variety of letters, so
contrary and barbarous to our pronunciation, as there are in Tamil (as we
shall see in the second reason) ; therefore, I have decided not to follow
the example of the others. As for my deviating from the natural Tamil
order, I answer that I am not writing this for Tamilians, but for the
Portuguese, for whom the natural order is the one that I follow, all the
more since the order which is most natural for the vocabulary is the one
that is easiest.
