97, Xutrea “Apud Xutres significa as partes
secretas, modesté (Among the Sudras
it means the private parts, modestly)

Goren (22)

See Shorter OED, IJ, 2070, s.v. “Sudra”
meaning “a member of the lowest of
the four great Hindu castes.” See also
Webster's 2519 with derivation from
Sanskrit Sudru. See also Proenga, 101.

98. yattiam “Huma bebida de eruas amargozas
no sacfifficig yattiam.” (A drink of
bitter herbs at the yattiam sacrifice).

Qeruurearo (363)

The word defined is Tamil céma-pdnam,
“drinking of the Soma beverage at the
Sdma sacrifice.” (Lexicon III, 672).
There may be a connection with “ yati,”
meaning “an ascetic, devotee,” derived
from Sanskrit (Webster's 2967), or with
Tamil ydttirai, “festival, dance.’ (Lexi-
con, VI, 3399),

99. Yucti “ Yucti, engého, conserto, item rezaé,
que se d&.” (Yucti, plan, agreement, also
reason, which is given).

wy eH (121)
This is a romanisation of Tamil yukti
(Lexicon, VI, 3408) or yutti (Lexicon,
VI, 3409), meaning “ plan, artifice,” etc.
