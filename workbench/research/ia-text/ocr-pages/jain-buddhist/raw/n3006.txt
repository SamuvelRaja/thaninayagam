“The darling daughter of the mountain chief,
She is well guarded ; access to her is hard.
Thy words of love she cannot understand.
Hence do not think of her”: thus, friend, thou say’st
On Kolli hill whose high and faultless top
The Gods protect and where the jak trees yield
Fruits on their red roots and white cascades fall,
A statue stands whose beauteous form remains
Untarnished, whether gales blow or hail bursts,
Or fiercely thunder rolls or other dangers
Rise or the world itself is wroth. My love,
Fair like that statue, cannot part from my heart.
