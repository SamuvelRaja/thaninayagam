BUDDHIST DHARMA
The Manimeékalai is uncompromisingly Buddhist and
offers more data concerning the content and methods of
Buddhist religious adult education than does the Silappati-
karam about Jain education. It is manifestly an epic of
the Hinayana doctrine, but it may be disputed as to which
of the Hinayana schools it belongs. There is no reference
in it to the Mahasanghika school which prevailed in the
Andhra country north of the Tamil border. Nor are there
references to Nagarjuna’s Madhyamika school, or to Arya-
deva or to any of the Mahayanist Yogacara teachers like
Dinnaga and Dharmapala who subsequently taught in
Kanchi. The Manimékalai probably belongs to the Saut-
rantika school of the Theravada and represents the Bud-
dhism prevailing in the Tamil country at the time of its
composition (circa a.p. 200 ?).%
