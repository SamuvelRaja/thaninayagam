LANGUAGES OF ADMINISTRATION
_ The stagnation in Tamil Culture that has been noted
before is not a little due to the want of State patronage
during the last three or four hundred years. With the
dawn of a new era in our national life, it is but legitimate
for us to expect the State to extend the same measure of
support to the development of the culture of the two
major nationalities that form the Ceylonese nation.
