IDEALS OF LIFE

Tamil Literature was the result of the Weltanschauung,
the world outlook of the Tamil-speaking peoples, and at
the same time that literature kept alive the outlook and
those ideals which shaped it. Imagination is a gift which
has been associated with great commercial peoples, and
no people in this part of the world were such skilful
navigators or traders as the Tamils.

The sea ports of the then Tamil country, which
included all the Malabar coast as well, were busy ports of
call into which ships from the West sailed with their gold,
lamps, wine and goblets, to return home laden with pepper
and silks and cotton and ivory, and with the pearls of the
Tamil seas. Teak from the Tamil country has been found
in the ruins of Ur of the Chaldees, and peacocks and apes
of the South were sold abroad as early as Solomon’s time.
