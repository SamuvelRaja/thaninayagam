““We should not forget,” concludes Thiru V.

This was the religious evolution that took place .
in the Tamil Nad. The worship of God receiving a
local colouring and a local name led later on to people
conceiving each separate region as having a separate
god, and the confusion was complete when puranic —
legend and Tamil superstition and folklore so combi-

y hed as to bury the original truth under mountainous
error. Tirumal or Mayon has been represented as a
shepherd. The Paripadal contains a few of the
devotional odes that were composed in his honour, and
gives us passages of exceptional beauty in which the
god is said to inform the entire universe.

Poems with the pastoral region as background
refer to him with great love and devotion. While
the worship of Murugan in the hill region emphasizes
an element of fear, the worship of Tirumal emphasi-
