These poems present faithful portraits of the
social, economic, political, and literary state of the
Tamil country, the two centuries before and after
Christ. A good portion of the literary legislation
of Tolkappiyam regarding Nature as the background
of poetry is illustrated by these poems. The Tolkap-

_ piyam and these poems which collectively are often
referred to as “Cangam Literature,” are the only
texts available for the present study.’
