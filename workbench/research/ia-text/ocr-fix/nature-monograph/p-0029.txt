e The entire passage in English runs: ‘‘ Come
hither, O fair boy ; for thee lo, the Nymphs bring
baskets full of lilies ; for thee the white Naiad plucks
pale violets and poppy heads, and adds the narcissus
and the fragrant anise-flower, and entwining them
with casia and other sweet-scented herbs, spangles
soft hyacinth posies with yellow marigold. Myself
will gather quinces with delicate silvery bloom, and
the chestnuts that my Amaryllis loved, and waxen
plums withal, this fruit likewise shall have his honour,
and you will I pluck, O laurels, and thee, bordering
myrtle, since so set you mingle your fragrant sweets.”
