Besides the usual Cangam verses cited in support
of the ancient Tamil belief in a personal Supreme
God, the word Kadavul itself, found in the earliest
Tamil works extant, is proof of the Tamils having
acquired early notions of God’s transcendence and
immanence. With regard to Nature, God is its Crea-
tor, and from him came the five elements and the
worlds.*
