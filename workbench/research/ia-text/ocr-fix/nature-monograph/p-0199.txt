- Scotland nursed many genuine poets of Nature,
and irregular, wild and mountainous country as it is,
it bred many a poet with a feeling for montane Nature.
Among them Burns has a foremost place, and he is
interesting to us because of the manner in which he
gave man the first place, and Nature, the second place,
and the mode in which he wove Nature and lovers.
