Naiure and Folklore
We have seen the use made of garlands and
flowers in warfare, and the different flowers that
signified different strategic movements. It remains
to sum up briefly those customs connected with
Nature which have not been mentioned so far.
Hach king and chief had a tree which symbolized
him and was called his guardian tree (sais) tot).
He seems to have planted his outer defences with
many trees of that species, and any king waging war
against another was supposed to score a great victory
over his enemy and disgrace him if he could penetrate
into the forest defences of his enemy, and cut down
his symbolic tree. Thus the margosa tree was the
“guardian tree” of Palayan and the punnai of
Titian. It was also counted an act of defiance and
bravery, if a king tied one of his elephants to the .
“* ouardian tree ” in the forest defences of his enemy.*
