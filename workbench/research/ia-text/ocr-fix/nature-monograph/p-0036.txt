The mullai bowers of the pasture lands (Gums),
and the pergola in front of the houses of the marudam
regions (usr), and the groves adjoining the sea-shore
(arene), are frequently mentioned by the poets as the
meeting places of lovers or of social festivities.

occur wherever possible (sas, ue, wm) and with a
frequency that denotes how welcome the shade was to
people in the torrid zone. Even eyes of women are
