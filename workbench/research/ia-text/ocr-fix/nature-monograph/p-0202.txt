Similarly the Bhagavad-Gita has these lines :—
Of creations the beginning and the ending,
and also the middle am I, O Arjuna. Of Sciences, the
science concerning the SELF ; the speech of oratorsI ...
And whatsoever is the seed of all beings, that am I,
O Arjuna ; nor is there aught, moving or unmoving, that
may exist bereftof Me...
Whatsoever is glorious, good, beautiful. and mighty, under-
stand thow that to go forth from a fragrant of My .
splendour. /
The sentiments these Tamil and Sanscrit lines express
were confined to devotion; they did not influence
the secular poetry of the Tamils, nor did they cause
the introduction of new trends in their interpretation
of Nature.
