The very names of flowers and trees have been
used to the utmost advantage by the Tamil poets to
jend music to their verse which, like Greek and Latin,
is based on quantity. They took full advantage of
the names, and as in Vergil, there are entire lines
that are but strings of names, resounding musical
lines ; “lines pulsing with the incomparable joy of
the born poet in the joy of the singing, the sonorous
word, a delight which is the first requisite of his work
of art; lines that spring from this most sensuous,
most indispensable, element to flower in the magic
of ‘names’ which in their turn derive from the spiritual
might of man from his faculty to give names to per-
sons and to things.”? Tamil has not the ceremonial
pomp of Latin, but these names, so they seem to me,
contain such a wealth of vocalic sounds, that their
concatenation in poetic lines has a most pleasing
effect, besides providing evidence of the skill of the
poets themselves.
