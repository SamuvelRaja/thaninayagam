A tender heart in Tamil is spoken of as a “ moist ”
heart. Moisture signifies kindness and tenderness
as in the line :—

“ Regarding moisture, like water art thou.” (Patir ; 90,14).

The fecundity of the earth and its maternal kind-
ness to man made all the ancients speak of the earth
as a woman and mother. But Tamil poetry like
Sanserit poetry carried the comparison even further
and spoke of the mountains as the breasts: of Mother
Earth, the bamboos as her slim shoulders, the rivers
as her necklaces and her garlands, the ocean as her
foam-bordered garment.1 Of these Nature’s land-
marks Tamil poetry paid most attention to the rivers
since these were the source of fertility for the Tamil
land, a source that failed not even when the rains failed.
