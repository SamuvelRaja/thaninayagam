1. ‘The term “ Cangam literature ” is very widely used among the Tamils
to designate their ancient books written prior to, say, the third cen-
tury of the Christian era. The word Cangam here stands for ‘ Literary
Academy’ and these books are the works of poets who lived at a period
when the activities of a literary body regulated and set the standards
for Tamil literature. The persistent Tamil tradition speaks of three
academies, the first two of which were succeeded by geological uphea-
vals that caused the loss also of the literature of those epochs. What
has remained are mostly the books of the third epoch or of the third
academy, and now * Cangam” has by degrees come to mean par
ewcellence the third academy and the third epoch.
