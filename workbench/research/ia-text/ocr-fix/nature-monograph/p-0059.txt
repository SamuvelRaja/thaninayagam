When a king went to war, he wore garlands made of
his royal flowers, as well as the flower that signified
the particular kind of warfare in which his troops
and he were going to engage.

Garlands of the flowers emblematic of the royal
houses were used also to decorate the royal standards.
In the graphic account of Pandyan Nedunjelian
visiting the wounded at mid-night, it is said that the
general who preceded him pointing out the wounded
soldiers one by one, carried a halberd around which
was wound a garland of margosa flowers.”
