panorama of Nature is made the stage of human ac-

described as if in sympathy with the heroine, for the
sufferings and bleak aspect presented in Nature
reflect the bleak and forlorn condition of the heroine
who desires the hero’s return. In other poems,
Nature is presented as placing obstacles on the way

contrast, for the joy of the meeting of the lovers is
