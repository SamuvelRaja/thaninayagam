The God of the maritime tracts was Varunan.
The manner in which he was worshipped is quite
characteristic of the local colour that the cult assumed.
The fisher-folk planted the horn of a shark in the
sandy beach and offered gifts of fresh and salted fish,
and bathed in the sea to rid themselves of their sins.
The shark was associated with Varunanas the elephant
was associated with Murugan. It was the strongest
denizen of the deep with which they had to contend.
Great feasts were also celebrated in honour of the sea-
god.’

the rains, the people conceived him as the rain-god
and the master of the clouds. The rivers too of Tamil
Nad were fed by the rains on the Western Ghats
