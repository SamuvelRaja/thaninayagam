Tirumal was worshipped in the pastoral regions
but his shrine of pilgrimage in the Cangam period
was Tirumal-irunjolai (the dark, dense, or large grove
of Tirumal) situated on a sober range of low evergreen
hills to the north of Madura. This locality combines
proximity to Madura with accessibility and natural
beauty, and was probably in origin a temple of the
shepherds of the region.
