4, Flos Sanctorum:

While the other three publications limit themselves
to the prayers and the articles of faith and the ex-
planation of basic doctrines, the Flos Sanctorum represents
the more ambitious attempt to publish in Tamil a sustained
work of considerations of the principal mysteries and
feasts, and the lives of the principal saints of the Church’s
Calendar. As a single work of considerations on the prin-
cipal feasts of the year, it does not seem that this work of
669 pages has been greatly surpassed in Catholic Tamil
Literature during the last four hundred years.

The Tamil title page and the contents in Tamil are
not to be found in the Vatican copy. There are, however,
four pages of a handwritten introduction in Spanish and
the table of contents in Spanish. Since the first page of
the Tamil text has the Arab numeral “8” printed in a
corner, it is probable that seven Pages of printed introduc-
tory matter preceded the text.
