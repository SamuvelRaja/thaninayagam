Christian community among whom the Franciscans worked
existed in Colombo in the sixteenth century. A Tamil-
speaking Catholic poet living in Colombo composed a prose
catechism to be chanted and some canticles to be sung
under the inspiration of Fra Joam, and Fra Joam sent to
Mannar from Colombo “a priest of the country well-versed
in Tamil” to help Fr. Henriques in the earlier period of
his Tamil studies.”
