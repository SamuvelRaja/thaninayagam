Universities, particularly those of Annamalai and
Madras, have periodically published works that are
most useful to foreign students interested in Tamil
studies. But it will not be sufficient to consign Tamil
studies only to Universities. Private institutes and
private agencies must as well take up the diffusion of
Tamil Culture. And that diffusion cannot hope to
achieve success unless the centres of Tamil research
set before themselves the highest standards of scholar-
ship? The more such scholarship embarks on compara-
tive studies, the greater will be its benefit to the world
at large. There is need for the disinterested and dispas-
sionate activities of an Academy which will not relegate
exclusively to Universities and the State the patronage
of Tamil studies.
