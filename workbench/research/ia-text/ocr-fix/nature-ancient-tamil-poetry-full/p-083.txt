of her lover who has not kept his tryst. The inference
is that since he has not kept his word he eannot be
an upright man, and consequently Nature cannot
appear lovely in his land.
“What! The river glimmers! Can it truly glimmer

in the land of one who is not true to his promises, who has

not kept his word? What! the rain cloud wanders,

does truly the rain cloud wander in the land of one who is

the cause of wasting form?”
