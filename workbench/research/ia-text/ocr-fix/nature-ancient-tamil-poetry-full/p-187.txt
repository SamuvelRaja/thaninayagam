Here are lines from his first canto on Sunimer,
and these may be compared with the Tamil poems on
the palai region :—

“Tracellers, whose hearts are scorched by the fire of
separation from their sweethearts, cannot even look at the
earth wherein columns of dust are raised by unbearable
gusts (of wind) (and) which is heated by the blaze of the
Sierce sun, “
Exceedingly scorched by the fierce sunshine, their palates
dried up by great thirst, the beasts have started off to another
forest.

Greatly scorched by the rays of the sun, a herd of wild
boars are as it were entering the earth, digging by means
of rows of long-stretching snows a pond wherein the mud
has dried up and (only) the bhadva musta grass remains.
All birds ave panting, sitting on trecs whose leaves have
fallen off; troops of Satigued monkeys are resorting to
mountain-caverns.

Everywhere the ground has been burnt by the wild fire...
The conflagration starting on the skirts torments all beasts ;
inereasing with the wind it burns in the mountain valleys ;
it breaks forth with sharp noises in places abounding with
dry bamboos, growing strong in a moment it spreads in the
grass,

Elephants, oxen and Kons, their bodies scorched together
by fire have flocked together like friends .. - they rest
by @ river with broad sandy banks,”
