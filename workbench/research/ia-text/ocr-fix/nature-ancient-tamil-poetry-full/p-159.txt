The lights of the fishing boats far out at sea
at night may be seen by those on shore, and the fisher-
men too out at sea may count such lights of city and
town as keep on burning late into the night. The
lights are as bright as the morning sun rising over
the brim of the ocean, On moonlit nights, the nets
lie on the shore like “ pieces of darkness,” and the
white sands and the sea-side groves seem like the
meeting of light and darkness.

The fisher maid during day keeps wateh in

drying. She scares away the birds that come to
feed on the fish. It is then that her lover goes to
meet her. The fishing village consists of small
huts. There is a smell of fish in the air. Nearby
