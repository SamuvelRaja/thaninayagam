The puram division corresponding to palai is
vahai named after the white crest-like flower of the
vahai tree, the mimosa flectuosa, one of the shadiest
in the landscape, but dreary-looking in summer.
The significance of this division is victory achieved
in war or in any of the other laudable walks of life,
victory that entitles one to the praise and glory of
his fellow-men. The vahat garland was worn by
armies when they returned victorious from the battle-
field, The reason for the desert being chosen was,
as the temporary desert may occur in any of the other
four regions, victory too may be obtained in any of
the other four strategic movements. While the other
four puram divisions are exclusively limited to war-
fare, the vahai division extended to the victories
of both war and peace, and could include also any
eulogistie poem.*
