The mention of the heroine’s father in poems like
this are intended to bring home to her that they are
yet within her father’s realm, and that she must
hasten to cross the borders of his kingdom, if her

relatives are not to overtake them in their flight.
The behaviour of beast and bird in the jungle
and the temporary desert are touching manifestations
of how love supports even the direst hardship, and
that life together amid the hardest of circumstances
is preferable to wealth and comfort obtained at the
cost of separation. The eleventh Kali ode, like se-
veral other poems contains, suggestive references.
