A story is recounted of birds in the person of Ay
Einan. He was so much a lover of birds and their
protector, that when he fell in the field of battle,
all the birds formed a canopy with their outstretched
wings to protect him from the rays of the sun. And,
it is added in the poems, that the owl was struck
with grief at its own want of vision during the day
for it could neither see Ay and his wounds, nor join
the other birds in providing shelter.’
