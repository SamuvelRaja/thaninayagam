Puram poetry was not limited by natural lands-

consider as its essential topic (2M Gum@sir) one of

annual season and the period of day pertinent to the
division, The poet had to be particular about choos-
ing similes and metaphors from objects (aga Gumgsir)
exclusive to the region. The objects were :—
the god, food, fauna, flora, musie and other objects
indigenous to the region. By way of exception the
flora or birds or any of the other objects of one region
may be mentioned in a poem of another region, for
even Nature is not rigorous in her natural divisions
as to be so exclusive. This mixing of ‘“ seed-topies ”
was permitted, and was known as “regional inter-
change” (Seer wuisio). Only there could be no

interchange as regards the gods ; they were exclusive

to one region."
