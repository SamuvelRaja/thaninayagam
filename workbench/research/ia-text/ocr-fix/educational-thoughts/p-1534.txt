The bards being panegyrists by profession were extre-
mely skilled in the use of words, and when poets gained
ascendancy in society their institution degenerated in
certain ways. They are used in the poems of a subsequent
stage of development as. conventional pimps and as com-
panions to young men in love, obviously because of. their
gifts of persuasive speech. A poem in the kuruntogai ex-
presses the marvel of a damsel at a bardic messenger’s flow
of language which she argued should be far more copious
in his own village if he could be so eloquent before strangers
In a strange place. (Kuruntogai ; 33). The expression
