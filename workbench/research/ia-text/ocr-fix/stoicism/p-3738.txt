Rhys Davids has considerable evidence to
support him when in one of his erudite and
searching introductions to the Suttas, he says,

‘‘Had the Buddha’s views on the whole
question won the day and widely shared as
they were by others, they very nearly pre-
vailed—the evolution of social grades and
distinctions would have gone on in India on
lines similar to those it followed in the West.
and the caste-system of India would never
have been built up.” #°

Some of the arguments used in the Nikayas
against caste prerogatives are most amusing as
when it is said oxen are not classified by their
colour but by their strength and activity, and
so, too should honours be conferred on men
according to ability, and if a sudra were wealthy,
the brahmins would be there to serve him. *: It
all sounds so much like the arguments used in
this twentieth century by platform orators in
India in the reform and self-respect movements.
