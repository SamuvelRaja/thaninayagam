The broder solidarity in cosmopolitanism is
generally strengthened by the loyalty to a parti-
cularistic national society, and in Tamil thought
a triple loyalty is implied, one to a particular
Tamil kingdom, the second to a citizenship in
the pan-Tamilism of three kingdoms; and a
third, the citizenship of the word.

differs, and the multilingual foreign groups of
