CONCLUSION

These comparisons of Indian thought with
Roman Stoicism may be made not only in the
domain of ethics but also in several other fields.
Three great books of Indian ethical thought
illustrate three great traditions, and point to a
certain unity as well as diversity within the
complex systems which come under Indian
thought. They are the Bhaguvad Gita, the
Dhammapada, and the Tirukkural; the first
theistic and incorporating several traditions, the
second representing the “sramanic” tradition of
asceticism and Buddhist in inspiration, and the
third humanistic, representative of a poetic tradi-
tion, and of a society in which priests and monks
have as yet no traditional social functions.
