To Tamil thought more than to any other
section of contemporary Indian thought, the
ideal of a Wise Man of human proportions with
human qualities was fundamental. He may not
have any of the thirty-two great marks, or the
numerous smaller marks which designate the
superman or Mahapurusa of other traditions.
He may not be endowed with the physical
qualities of the magnanious man of Aristotle.

confound physical appearance with personality
