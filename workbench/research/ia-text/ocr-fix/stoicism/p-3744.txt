ancient literatures for ethical values, was quick
to perceive the contribution which Tamil thought
had to make by emphasising this ethic of love.
Of the Tirukkural, a codex of gnomic verse which
epitomises the ethical Tamil thought of this
period, he says that its ethics is more advanced
than that of Brahminism, Buddhism, and Bhagvad-
gita) Hinduism.
