# Biography audit: achievements, conferences, journals, and early print

Reviewed 8 October 2026. This is a read-only audit; website copy was not changed.

The sole substantive source is Fr. D. Amudhan Adigal, *தவத்திரு தனிநாயகம் அடிகளார்*, International Institute of Tamil Studies, July 1993. References below use the book's **printed page numbers** and the **one-based Markdown line numbers** in [the converted biography](/Users/samuvelraja/dev/Freelance/thaninayagam/public/books/TVA_BOK_0009080_தனிநாயகம்_அடிகளார்.md). The original scan (now retained outside the repository) was visually checked at printed pp. 66, 67, 70, 108, and 187 (physical PDF pages 101, 102, 105, 143, and 222). That check confirms the participant count, elected officers, conference date and office, journal interruptions, and language-count qualification.

**Supported** means explicit in this source; **contradicted** means materially incompatible evidence; **not located** means this source does not establish the precise detail, without declaring the claim false; **ambiguous** means qualified or internally differing evidence. The biography sometimes distinguishes firm documentation from inference and recollection; this audit preserves that distinction.

Scope includes `CONTRIBUTORS_PILLARS`, related publishing and conference statements in `BIO_SECTIONS` and `MILESTONES`, institution/conference `KEY_FACTS`, relevant `JOURNEY_STOPS`, both contributions pages, `LivingHero`, `LivingMetrics`, `JourneyGrid`, `FactGrid`, and retained `OriginalHomeIntro`.

The current rendered homepages use **LivingMetrics and JourneyGrid**. **OriginalHomeIntro is dormant code**; its fuller claims are latent issues, not current homepage copy. JourneyGrid displays the year and place of each stop, but not its note. Thus its 1950 Lisbon and 1969 Paris/Naples stops remain visible issues even though the detailed descriptions are hidden.

## Main editorial priorities

1. Qualify current homepage metrics: country for 200+ talks, evidence level for language count, journal interruptions rather than fourteen continuous years.
2. Reconcile current journey dates: the source does not establish the 1950 Lisbon discovery, and dates Paris/Naples appointments to 1970/1971.
3. Correct office/count conflations in contributions data: conference deputy chair versus IATR secretary, 26 people versus 25+ countries, and the final elected vice-president list.
4. Separate early books' print locations, copy locations, manuscript status, and research/preservation roles.
5. Preserve unresolved edition/date differences as questions for corroboration rather than replacing them with new unsupported certainty.
