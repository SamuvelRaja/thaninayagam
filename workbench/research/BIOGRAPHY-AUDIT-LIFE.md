# Biography source audit: life, education, chronology, and About pages

Audit date: 8 October 2026. Read-only audit of the website; no website copy was changed.

Source: `public/books/TVA_BOK_0009080_தனிநாயகம்_அடிகளார்.md`, Fr. D. Amudhan Adigal, *தவத்திரு தனிநாயகம் அடிகளார்* (1993), with its corresponding PDF used to check uncertain OCR and printed pagination. Website scope: `lib/data.ts` (`KEY_FACTS`, `JOURNEY_STOPS`, `BIO_SECTIONS`, `MILESTONE_ERAS`, `MILESTONES`) and `app/about/page.tsx`, `app/en/about/page.tsx`.

“Supported” means this biography supports the claim, not that it has been independently verified against every other historical source. “Contradicted” means the source gives a materially different statement. “Not located” means the biography does not establish the detail; it is not proof that the detail is false. “Ambiguous” means the wording, OCR, source qualifications, or distinctions between events prevent a clean confirmation. Main narrative chapters take precedence over loose reminiscences in the foreword.

References below use **printed book pages**, followed by **exact Markdown line numbers**. In the front matter, Markdown comment `[பக்கம் 28]` is the scan position, whereas the PDF actually prints **xxvii**. Physical PDF page 28 = printed xxvii; physical PDF page 234 = printed 199. Arabic printed pages map to physical PDF page + 35. The source PDF was visually checked at printed xxvi, xxvii, 199, and 201 during this audit.

## Material corrections

1. Parents and birth name differ from the website. Printed p. 1 names **ஹென்ரி ஸ்தனிஸ்லாஸ் கணபதிப்பிள்ளை** and **செசில் இராசம்மா வஸ்தியாம்பிள்ளை**, and says the given name was **சேவியர்**, used with his father's name as **சேவியர் ஸ்தனிஸ்லாஸ்** (MD 288). The source does not establish “Nicholas” or father “Naganathan.”
2. **M.A. in 1947, M.Litt. in 1949**, with **A. Chidambaranathan** as research supervisor (p. 11, MD 418). The website repeatedly says M.Litt. 1945–1947 under S. Somasundara Bharati.
3. The Vadakangulam school is **St. Theresa's High School**, where he was deputy headmaster (pp. 7–8, MD 368–378), not St. Joseph's.
4. **Tamil Literature Society was founded in 1948**, **Tamil Culture began in February 1952**, and **Academy of Tamil Culture was founded in Chennai in 1954**. The current copy collapses these into one 1952 Tuticorin foundation.
5. Paris teaching was at **Collège de France in 1970**; Naples teaching was in **1971** (p. 199, MD 2477; p. 189, MD 2369). “Sorbonne and Naples, 1969” is inconsistent with the biography.
6. He **headed**, rather than founded, Malaya's Indian Studies department: **Muthu Rasakkannanar headed it in 1960–1961** before him (p. 46, MD 810).
7. The **1981 Madurai statue** is supported, but the doctorate narrated in the source is from **Tamil University**, at a subsequent convocation after its 15 September 1981 opening; **University of Jaffna, 1981** is not established (printed xxvi–xxvii, MD 202–208).
8. The 1966 conference organising committee made him **deputy chairman**, while **V. Selvanayagam was general secretary** (p. 70, MD 1082). The website calls Thani Nayagam conference general secretary.
9. The Tamil claim of IATR formation with scholars from **25+ countries** is inconsistent with the actual **26 people** founding roster (pp. 66–67, MD 1029–1043). The English 26-scholars claim is supported.
10. The source places the **200-plus lectures in the United States in one year**, not across America, Europe, and Asia or solely at universities (p. 13, MD 459; pp. 14–15, MD 471–475).
