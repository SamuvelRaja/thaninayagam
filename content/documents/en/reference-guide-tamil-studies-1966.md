## Bibliographic record

Amudhan records compilation during 1961–1963, publication in 1966, and 1,355 book records. The narrative gives 122 numbered pages; the bibliography gives vi + 122 (printed pp. 178–179, 211). The independently linked catalogue gives viii + 122. That preliminary-page discrepancy requires edition verification rather than replacing numbered pages with a total. The subtitle *Books* distinguishes this volume from the 1961 journal guide.
