## Bibliographic record

Amudhan’s narrative calls this Tamil travel-essay collection *ஒன்றே உலகம்*, first published by Pari Nilayam in 1963, with a revised second edition in 1974. His bibliography also lists a 1966 Pari Nilayam issue, 230 p. (printed pp. 149, 214). The existing catalogue uses the title variant *ஒரே உலகம்*. This record distinguishes the work’s first publication from the 1966 issue.
