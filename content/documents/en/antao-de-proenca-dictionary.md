## Bibliographic record

Xavier S. Thani Nayagam, “Antao De Proenca’s Tamil-Portuguese Dictionary, 1679,” *Tamil Culture*, Vol. XI No. 2 (Apr.–Jun. 1964), pp. 117–127, with an English translation of Proença’s preface by Edgar C. Knowlton, Jr. and Thani Nayagam (pp. 128–134), and Knowlton’s companion article “Antao de Proença’s Vocabulario Tamulico Lusitano: Indo-Portuguese Elements” (pp. 135–163).
