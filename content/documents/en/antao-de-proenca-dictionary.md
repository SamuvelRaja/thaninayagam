## Bibliographic record

Thani Nayagam’s introduction to Proença’s 1679 Tamil–Portuguese dictionary appeared in *Tamil Culture* XI.2 (April–June 1964), pp. 117–127. The preface translation with Edgar C. Knowlton Jr. follows on pp. 128–134. Amudhan distinguishes those articles from the dictionary edition prepared for publication in 1966 (biography pp. 132, 210–211).

## Reading here

The English introduction and translated preface follow, with Knowlton’s companion Indo-Portuguese study (pp. 135–163). This is a journal dossier, not the full 1966 dictionary. Consult the scan for Tamil headwords and Portuguese spellings.
