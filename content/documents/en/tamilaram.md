## Bibliographic record

*Tamilāram* is a volume of tributes and reminiscences dedicated to the memory of Father Thani Nayagam, edited by Rajan Philipupillai and published by Theepam Institute (Jaffna). Digitized by Noolaham Foundation.

## Biography context

The biography cites colleagues’ recollections in Tamilāram, Jaffna, 1983 (printed p. 220), supporting its memorial character. Its editor/publisher credits here come from the separate volume record, rather than an editor credit in the biography. It is not an original book authored by Thani Nayagam.
