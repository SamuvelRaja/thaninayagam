## Bibliographic record

This selection contains *Ancient Tamil Literature and the Study of Ancient Indian Education* (1956), *The Educators of Early Tamil Society* (1956), and *Ancient Tamil Poet-Educators* (1957). Amudhan lists them on printed pp. 207–208. His unpublished London thesis is the singular *Educational Thought in Ancient Tamil Literature* (1957; ix + 482 p.), a separate work (biography pp. 174, 208, 219).
