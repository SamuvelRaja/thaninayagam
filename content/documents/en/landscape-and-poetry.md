## Bibliographic record

Amudhan records *Landscape and Poetry: A Study of Nature in Classical Tamil Poetry*, Asia Publishing House, Bombay, 1966 (biography pp. 171, 211). This catalogue retains the independently listed 1967 edition date. The 1966/1967 edition relationship and the later IITS reprint require their own edition evidence. The biography connects Landscape with the earlier nature-poetry study.
