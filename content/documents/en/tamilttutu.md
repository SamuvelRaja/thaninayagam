## Editions

Amudhan records the first edition in 1952, Tamil Literature Society, Tuticorin (110 numbered pages), a second edition in 1959, and the revised fourth edition in 1962, Pari Nilayam, Chennai (126 pages). These are edition dates, rather than competing dates for a single copy (biography pp. 134, 213).
