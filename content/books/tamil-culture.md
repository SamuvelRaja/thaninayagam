## About this holding / இப்பதிவு பற்றி

*Tamil Culture* was the English-language quarterly of Tamil studies that Thani Nayagam founded and edited through volumes 1–12 (1952–1966). The Internet Archive packages a bound scan of those volumes (4,768 leaves) under the item *Tamil Culture by Xavier Thaninayagam Adigalar Volume 1–12*.

This page is a **finding aid**, not a continuous reprint of that bound set. Many articles in the scan are by other contributors; the links below focus on Thani Nayagam’s essays that have been re-OCR’d and lightly cleaned for on-site reading.
