## Publication note

Open Library records *The Carthaginian clergy during the episcopate of Saint Cyprian* (Tamil Literature Society, Tuticorin, 1950; xviii, 112 p.), from Thani Nayagam’s Roman doctoral research on St Cyprian (248–258 AD). An American souvenir edition appeared around his 1950 lecture tour.

Internet Archive item `bwb_W8-ANY-806` is available only as controlled lending — borrow the scan there; this site does not host the full text.

## Memorial-volume context

Memorial accounts describe the thesis as growing out of his study of the catacombs and early Church history in Rome, published as *The Carthaginian Clergy* — a study of St Cyprian’s episcopate and clergy (248–258 AD). That early-Church research is often linked to his later concern that the Church be “made incarnate” in the cultures it entered, and to his founding of a Veeramamunivar (Beschi) Society in Rome to promote Tamil among Tamilian students there.

For the related nature-poetry line that followed his Annamalai degrees, see *Nature in Ancient Tamil Poetry* and *Landscape and Poetry* on this archive.
